# Reflex Sprint - 30-Day Agentic Adoption Sprint Builder

A guided builder for managers and team leads who want to drive AI agent adoption through a personalized 30-day sprint.

## What it does
- Teaches the 6 change reflexes + 3 micro-practices
- Walks leaders through a 5-step Design Wizard with multi-select focal reflexes and a Design-your-own option per week
- Generates a Plan Summary with a 30-day calendar grid
- Exports the plan to PDF and to an Outlook .ics calendar invite
- Tracks weekly evidence (Win / Friction / Decision)
- Conditional Friction Log, Evidence capture, and Scale-Stop-Shift decisions
- Auto-generated Leader Evidence Pack with totals
